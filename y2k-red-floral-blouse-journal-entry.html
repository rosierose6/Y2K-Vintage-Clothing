5 Ways to Style a Red Floral Y2K Blouse

Journal · 4 min read

A v-neck, three-quarter sleeves, a red floral print loud enough to be the whole point — this blouse doesn't need much help. Here's how we'd wear it through the week.

one

Tucked into black trousers

Tuck it fully into wide-leg black trousers and let the v-neck and the print do the work. Gold hoops and nothing else — this outfit doesn't need more decisions.

two

Knotted over a slip dress

Layer it open over a slip dress in black or cream and knot it at the waist. It adds structure to something soft without covering up either piece.

three

With denim and nothing else loud

Low-rise jeans, white sneakers, done. The blouse is already a statement — everything else should disappear into the background.

four

Half-buttoned over a white tank

Leave it open with only the bottom two buttons closed, worn over a plain white tank. It reads more like a light jacket than a top, which makes it easier to layer into fall.

five

As a dress, belted

If it runs long enough on you, belt it at the natural waist and wear it as a dress with tights and boots. One piece, a completely different outfit.
