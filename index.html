About the Shop

Slow Fashion,
One Treasure at a Time

Thrifted by hand,
listed with care

Y2K Vintage Clothing is a small curated shop for one-of-a-kind Y2K, vintage & fairycore pieces. Every item is thrifted by hand, cleaned, measured, and photographed before it's listed — so what you see is exactly what arrives.

Because everything is secondhand, each piece is one of one. If something speaks to you, it won't be restocked. All purchases, questions, and offers happen through Depop, @fairytqles.
