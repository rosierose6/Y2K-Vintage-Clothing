5 Ways to Style a Coquette Ribbon Bow Sweater This Fall

A Y2K Vintage Clothing Styling Guide · 4 Min Read

Every so often a piece comes in that feels like it was made for exactly one girl — this one's the Japanese ribbon bow ruffle collar sweater. It's soft, it's a little precious, and it works a lot harder than a "one outfit" piece has any right to. Here's how we'd wear it.

With low-rise denim, undone

The original pairing, and still the best one. Let the ruffle collar and bow sit high while the waistband sits low — the contrast is the whole outfit. Keep shoes simple (ballet flats or beat-up sneakers) so the sweater stays the focal point.

Layered over a mesh tank

Unbutton it halfway and let a green floral mesh tank peek out underneath. It reads a little more fairycore, a little less coquette — proof this sweater doesn't only live in one aesthetic.

Tucked into a mini skirt

Tuck the front only, leave the back loose. It's an easy way to add shape without losing the sweater's soft, slouchy silhouette — try it with something in cherry red or gingham for a proper 2003 moment.

Styled with cargo pants for contrast

Ribbon bows and utility pockets shouldn't work together, and that's exactly why they do. It softens the cargo pants, and the cargo pants keep the sweater from feeling too costume-y.

As the whole outfit, with statement shoes

Some pieces don't need help. Wear it as a dress with opaque tights and platform mary janes, and let the bow do all the talking.
